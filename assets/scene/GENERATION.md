# Warsaw wall artwork

Created with built-in imagegen to replace the original San Francisco scene in an earlier homepage. The generated original was `warsaw-palace.png`; the optimized page asset was `warsaw-palace.webp`. That version retained the existing frame and darkened the illustration with CSS stars in night mode. Both files are now preserved in the external [art archive](../../docs/art-archive.md); the current homepage's Warsaw picture is part of the workstation artwork.

Architectural reference details were checked against the [National Heritage Institute](https://zabytek.pl/en/obiekty/palac-kultury-i-nauki-warszawa) and the [Palace of Culture and Science website](https://pkin.pl/en/trivia/). This is an artistic cityscape, not a documentary photograph or exact architectural survey.

## Exact prompt

Use case: illustration-story.
Asset type: landscape illustration inside a small framed wall picture in an existing hand-drawn office website.
Primary request: an immediately recognizable illustrated view of Warsaw, Poland, centered on the Palace of Culture and Science (Pałac Kultury i Nauki).
Scene: cheerful clear daytime Warsaw cityscape. The Palace of Culture and Science is the dominant large central subject: pale warm beige stone, its iconic broad symmetrical monumental stepped base, four lower corner annexes, tall rectangular tiered central tower with many small rhythmic windows, decorative Polish Renaissance-inspired parapets and pinnacles, prominent square clock section near the summit, long slender metal spire rising from the clock-tower crown. Accurate recognizable silhouette, not a church or medieval castle. Palace fills most of image height, the entire spire visible with a little sky above. Supporting modern blue-gray glass Warsaw skyscrapers off to either side at lower visual prominence, a few small green trees at the base and a hint of the open square in foreground. No San Francisco buildings, no Golden Gate Bridge, no mountains.
Style: playful sophisticated hand-inked editorial cartoon to match a personal website's illustrated desk scene. Strong black contour lines, simple clean shapes, small penciled hatch shadows, warm cream architecture, light blue sky with a few white rounded clouds, restrained greens and cool blue modern buildings. Crisp, readable at approximately 263 by 192 pixels. No watercolor washes, no realism, no 3D.
Composition: landscape approximately 11:8 aspect ratio (around 1376 by 1000). Center the Palace of Culture and Science horizontally, all of its top spire and wide base inside the image. Front three-quarter architectural view, recognizable clock face. Draw artwork edge-to-edge with opaque sky/background, no border, no picture frame, no mat, no window mullions. This will be placed inside an existing dark frame in HTML.
No text, lettering, labels, watermark, people, or dogs.
