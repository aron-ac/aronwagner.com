# Scene artwork sources

The original desk and office props are by/from [Gregory Koberger's personal website](https://gkoberger.com/), downloaded on 2026-09-25 to reproduce the hero layout requested for this project. These historical files are copies of the referenced artwork; they are not newly generated illustrations. Original ownership remains with the respective creator. They are unused by the current site and tooling and are preserved in the external [art archive](../../docs/art-archive.md), under their original `assets/scene/` paths.

The following files were retrieved from `https://gkoberger.com/images/` using their identical local filenames:

`desk@2x.webp`, `whiteboard@2x.webp`, `window@2x.webp`, `books@2x.webp`, `headphones@2x.webp`, `mug@2x.webp`, `writing@2x.webp`, `laptop@2x.webp`, `dribbble@2x.webp`, `instagram@2x.webp`, `github@2x.webp`, and `map@2x.webp`.

The original, now unused San Francisco window scenery comes from `https://gkoberger.com/images/window/`; the local `window-` filename prefix was added to these originals:

`row1@2x.webp`, `row1-dark@2x.webp`, `row2@2x.webp`, `row3@2x.webp`, `ggb@2x.webp`, and `cloud@2x.webp`.

Layout measurements were inspected in [the original stylesheet](https://gkoberger.com/stylesheets/style.css). The original portrait and owl artwork are not included.

The subsequent wall picture was the custom-generated `warsaw-palace.webp`, centered on Warsaw's Palace of Culture and Science. That WebP and its original PNG are also archived. The exact built-in imagegen prompt remains in `GENERATION.md`. The current wall picture is embedded in the workstation scene.
