Create a landscape illustration, 1536x1024 pixels (3:2), for the homepage of a personal website.

IMAGE 1 is a STYLE REFERENCE ONLY. Match its hand-inked editorial cartoon style exactly: clear confident black ink outlines, flat colors with restrained pencil hatching, a cutaway "dollhouse" room seen from a shallow three-quarter view, open at the front, with no ceiling, and the whole room floating on a transparent background. Do NOT copy its room, furniture, people, posters or objects.

IMAGES 2–5 are photographs of the real home office to draw. Recreate THIS room faithfully as a cohesive cartoon vignette:

Room shell
- Deep hunter-green walls, with a chair rail, rectangular picture-frame wainscoting panels below it, and crown molding along the top edge, all painted the same green.
- Light oak wood floor with a large Persian rug in cream, rust and brown covering most of it.
- Show three wall planes like an open box: a back wall facing the viewer, a left wall and a right wall angled toward the viewer. No ceiling plane.

Back wall (center)
- A window with dark wood trim and white plantation shutters, glowing with soft daylight.
- In front of it, a dark espresso wood double-pedestal desk with brushed-nickel bar pulls and a black desk mat.
- On the desk: two large CURVED monitors side by side on monitor arms, a small silver Mac Studio, a black podcast microphone on a round stand between the monitors, a silver-and-black full-size keyboard, a white trackpad, a dark tablet in a folio case, and a small light-blue zip case.
- Draw BOTH monitor screens as plain, evenly glowing pale-blue panels with NO interface, text, windows or pictures on them. Their full rectangular outlines must be clearly visible and not blocked by anything.
- To the left of the window: two framed certificates stacked vertically (a black frame above, a wood frame below) with simplified, unreadable text.
- In the back-right corner: a tall black safe with a dial.
- A black mesh ergonomic office chair with a headrest, EMPTY, pulled back from the desk and turned slightly toward the viewer.

Left wall
- The built-in green bookcase from IMAGE 4: open cubbies on both sides of a center cabinet with two doors, and a row of drawers along the bottom.
- In the cubbies, spaced apart so each object is clearly separate: a vintage white Apple eMac computer, a black printer, woven baskets, a row of colorful books on one shelf, a small black leather Bible on another, a classic 1980s BMW scale model car in red on its own shelf, and a small instant camera on its own shelf.

Right wall
- The framed Army Class A uniform from IMAGE 5, drawn clearly: a dark blue Army Service Uniform jacket with sergeant chevrons, an 82nd Airborne "AA" shoulder patch, ribbons, jump wings and gold buttons, in a gold frame with a light-blue mat.
- Beside it, the framed "THE TAMPA TRIBUNE" newspaper with the headline "Man on the Moon", in a dark wood frame with a cream mat.
- A golf bag with a few clubs standing on the floor in the right front corner, clearly separate from other objects.

Composition
- Leave open rug space in the lower center foreground, in front of the desk. Characters will be added there later, so keep it clear.
- Keep every object fully visible and not overlapping its neighbors, so each can be clicked on the website.
- Warm, welcoming daytime lighting: the green walls read rich but not dark, the window glows, and the desk stays dark espresso.

Output
- A cutout room vignette with a TRANSPARENT background outside the walls and floor, about 3% empty margin on all sides, and no outer shadow, frame or page background.
- No people, no animals, no ceiling fan, no watermark, and no text anywhere except the newspaper headline.
