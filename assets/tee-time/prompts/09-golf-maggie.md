Using the attached character sheet as the exact reference for Maggie and the art style (same hand-inked cartoon look, line weight, shading and colors), draw a video game sprite sheet of Maggie, the curly apricot Goldendoodle.

- Side view, facing right, full body in every pose, all at exactly the same size and scale, standing on the same invisible ground line.
- Arrange six poses in a 3 × 2 grid, evenly spaced, not touching:
  1. Running to the right with a white golf ball in her mouth, front legs reaching forward.
  2. Running to the right with the golf ball in her mouth, legs gathered under her (the other half of the run).
  3. Running to the right with no ball, mouth open and happy, front legs reaching forward.
  4. Running to the right with no ball, legs gathered under her.
  5. Sitting proudly, facing the viewer, with the golf ball on the ground between her front paws.
  6. Play bow: front end down, tail up and wagging, golf ball on the ground in front of her nose.
- The golf ball is small and plain white with dimples, the same size in every pose.
- Transparent background. No floor, no shadows, no text, no borders.
- Wide image, 1536×1024.
