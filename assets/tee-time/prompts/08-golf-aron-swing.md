Using the attached character sheet as the exact reference for Aron and the art style (same hand-inked cartoon look, line weight, shading, colors and face), draw a video game sprite sheet of Aron playing golf.

- Outfit in every pose: black short-sleeve golf polo with a small grey American flag on the chest, khaki golf shorts, white golf shoes, no hat. Same face, short dark hair, and tattoo sleeves on both arms.
- Side view, facing right (the ball would be in front of his feet, to the right). Full body from head to shoes in every pose, all at exactly the same size and scale, standing on the same invisible ground line. He holds a driver in poses 1–3 and a putter in pose 4.
- Arrange six poses in a 3 × 2 grid, evenly spaced, not touching:
  1. Address: set up over the ball, club head resting on the ground in front of him.
  2. Top of the backswing: club raised high behind his head.
  3. Follow-through: finished swing, club over his shoulder, watching the ball fly.
  4. Putting stance: bent over a putter, eyes down.
  5. Celebrating: fist pump with a big grin, club in the other hand.
  6. Frustrated: one hand on his head, club resting on the ground, playful grimace (not angry).
- Transparent background. No ball, no floor, no shadows, no text, no borders.
- Wide image, 1536×1024.
