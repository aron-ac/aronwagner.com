Using the first attached illustration as the art style reference (same hand-inked cartoon look, line weight, soft shading and warm colors), and the second as the exact reference for Rebecca and baby Jack, draw a sheet of 20 props for a side-scrolling golf video game.

- Draw every object in a straight side view (profile), as seen from the side in a side-scrolling game, not from above.
- 5 columns × 4 rows, evenly spaced, each object centered in its own cell and filling most of it, not touching.
- Row 1 (inside the house): a wooden office desk with a computer monitor on it, a black office chair, a grey three-seat sofa, a low wooden coffee table, a kitchen island with two bar stools.
- Row 2 (inside the house): a small pile of colorful baby toy blocks, a round dog bed, a basket full of dog toys, a tall stainless-steel refrigerator, an open glass patio door in its frame.
- Row 3 (the golf course): a tall palm tree, a leafy oak tree, a golf flagstick with a red flag, a white golf cart, a wooden park bench.
- Row 4: an alligator's head and eyes peeking up out of water (with a little water around it), a pair of red golf tee markers, a black patio grill, a potted palm in a planter, and Rebecca (same outfit as the reference) holding baby Jack on her hip, both cheering.
- No logos, no brand names, no text anywhere.
- Transparent background. No ground, no shadows, no borders, no grid lines.
- Wide image, 1536×1024.
