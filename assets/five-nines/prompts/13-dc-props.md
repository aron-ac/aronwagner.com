Using the attached illustration as the art style reference (same hand-inked cartoon look, line weight, soft shading and colors), draw a sheet of 20 props and characters for a side-scrolling data center video game.

- 5 columns × 4 rows, evenly spaced, each item centered in its own cell and filling most of it, not touching.
- Row 1 (front view, facing the viewer): a small standing help desk with a computer monitor, keyboard and a desk phone; a rolling metal cart stacked with spare hard drives; a large wall-mounted TV screen that is turned off (plain dark screen); a red fire extinguisher hanging in a wall bracket; a red emergency button in a yellow box on a wall plate.
- Row 2: a coiled blue network cable; a single server hard drive; a stack of three pizza boxes; a coffee maker with a steaming mug on a small cart; a stack of cardboard shipping boxes.
- Row 3: five different customers, head and shoulders only, facing the viewer, each with a funny impatient or confused expression (not angry): a young developer with big headphones and a hoodie; a middle-aged man in a polo shirt holding a phone to his ear; a woman in glasses squinting at a laptop screen; a startup founder in a beanie and a puffy vest; a sweet grandmother with a knitting needle and yarn.
- Row 4: three cartoon fire flames of the same fire (small, medium and large) so they can flicker as an animation; a big white puff of fire-extinguisher foam; a puff of grey smoke.
- No logos, no brand names, no text or numbers anywhere.
- Transparent background. No floor, no shadows, no borders, no grid lines.
- Wide image, 1536×1024.
