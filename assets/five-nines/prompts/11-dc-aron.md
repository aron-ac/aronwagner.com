# Prompt 11 — Aron in the data center (10 poses)

Using the attached character sheet as the exact reference for Aron and the art style (same hand-inked cartoon look, line weight, shading, colors and face), draw a video game sprite sheet of Aron working in a data center.

- Outfit in every pose: black zip-up hoodie, unzipped, over his black T-shirt with the grey American flag, sleeves pushed up to the elbows so his tattoo sleeves show on both forearms, a blue lanyard with a blank white ID badge, dark jeans, dark grey sneakers. Same face and short dark hair.
- Side view, facing right. Full body from head to shoes in every pose, all at exactly the same size and scale, standing on the same invisible ground line.
- Arrange ten poses in a 5 × 2 grid, evenly spaced, not touching:
  1. Standing, relaxed.
  2. Walking, mid-stride with his left leg forward.
  3. Walking, mid-stride with his right leg forward.
  4. Running, big panicked stride, arms pumping.
  5. Reaching forward at chest height, plugging a network cable into something in front of him.
  6. Sliding a hard drive forward into a slot at chest height.
  7. Spraying a red fire extinguisher forward, bracing, determined face (no spray cloud).
  8. Typing on a keyboard at chest height in front of him, focused.
  9. Celebrating: fist pump with a big relieved grin.
  10. Stressed: both hands on his head, eyes wide, playful panic (not angry).
- Transparent background. No floor, no shadows, no text, no logos, no borders.
- Wide image, 1536×1024.
