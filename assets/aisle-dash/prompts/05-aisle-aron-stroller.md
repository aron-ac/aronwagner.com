Using the attached character sheet as the exact reference for the people and the art style (same hand-inked cartoon look, line weight, shading, colors and faces), draw a video game sprite sheet of Aron pushing a stroller with baby Jack in it.

- Side view, facing right. Full body from head to shoes, with the whole stroller visible.
- Aron: same face, short dark hair, black T-shirt with the grey American flag, dark jeans, tattoo sleeves on both arms, dark grey sneakers. Both hands on the stroller handle, relaxed and smiling.
- Jack: 9 months old with his full head of dark hair, sitting up in the stroller seat facing forward (to the right), grey-and-white striped onesie, happy.
- Stroller: modern dark grey stroller, canopy pulled back so Jack is clearly visible, storage basket underneath, black wheels.
- 3 frames in one row, evenly spaced, same size and alignment: frame 1 standing still, frame 2 mid-stride with his left leg forward, frame 3 mid-stride with his right leg forward. Aron, Jack and the stroller must look identical in every frame except Aron's legs.
- Transparent background. No floor, no shadows, no text, no borders.
- Wide image, 1536×1024.
