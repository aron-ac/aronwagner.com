# Prompt 06 — Rebecca's six poses

Using the attached character sheet as the exact reference for Rebecca and the art style (same hand-inked cartoon look, line weight, shading, colors and face), draw a video game sprite sheet of Rebecca in six poses.

- Same outfit in every pose: long brown hair, black sleeveless top, thin gold necklace, cream wide-leg pants, brown sandals.
- Full body from head to sandals in every pose, all at exactly the same size and scale, each standing on the same invisible ground line.
- Arrange the poses in a 3 × 2 grid, evenly spaced, not touching:
  1. Walking to the right, left leg forward.
  2. Walking to the right, right leg forward (same as pose 1 except the legs).
  3. Browsing: facing right, turned slightly toward the viewer, one hand reaching out to the right as if touching something on a shelf, curious smile.
  4. Delighted: facing the viewer, hands clasped near her chest, big happy smile.
  5. Unimpressed: facing the viewer, arms crossed, one eyebrow raised, small smirk. Playful, not angry.
  6. Happy with an iced coffee: facing the viewer, holding a clear plastic iced coffee cup with a straw.
- Transparent background. No floor, no shadows, no text, no borders.
- Wide image, 1536×1024.
