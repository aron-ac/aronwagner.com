# Character artwork

Created with the built-in imagegen tool for an earlier homepage. The supplied photo was used as the identity reference for Mark; a screenshot of https://gkoberger.com/ was used as the illustration-style reference. The white pomsky used the subsequently supplied dog photograph as its identity reference. Its files were `pomsky-white.png` and the optimized `pomsky-white.webp`; these superseded the original cream/gray `pomsky` files.

All six PNG/WebP files are unused by the current homepage and tooling and are preserved in the external [art archive](../../docs/art-archive.md), under their original `assets/characters/` paths. The current character artwork is composited into the workstation scene; the prompts below remain as design history.

## Portrait prompt

Use case: illustration-story.
Asset type: transparent character cutout for the hero of Mark Hammonds's personal website.
Input images: the attached photographic headshot is the identity reference for Mark; the screenshot of the cream-background illustrated office website is ONLY a drawing-style and pose reference.
Primary request: draw Mark, the man from the photographic headshot, as a friendly hand-drawn cartoon character in exactly the approachable ink-and-color editorial illustration style of the office scene. This is a standalone character sprite, NOT an entire website or office.
Subject: recognizable likeness with voluminous dark brown swept-up hair, closely trimmed sides, strong dark eyebrows, brown eyes, full neatly shaped dark beard and mustache, light warm skin, and the navy blazer and open-collar white shirt from the photo. Friendly slight closed-mouth smile. Keep the beard full and dark. No glasses.
Composition: front view, waist-up seated at a desk, head looking at viewer, shoulders and torso fully included, elbows bent with hands together in front at desk height, right hand resting casually near the left hand. Similar proportions and pose to the seated man in the office screenshot: head slightly exaggerated, torso below, enough space around silhouette. Character alone centered. Bottom edge may end at waist. No desk, no laptop, no chair, no objects, no animals.
Style: confident black hand-inked contour lines, flat colors with subtle pencil hatch shading, playful sophisticated cartoon, clean readable at 235px wide. Not 3D, not photorealistic, not anime.
Background: genuinely transparent alpha background, no checkerboard drawn into pixels, no white matte, no shadows outside silhouette.
No text, no watermark.

## White pomsky prompt

Use case: illustration-story.
Asset type: transparent illustrated dog sprite for Mark Hammonds's personal website.
Input image: the attached photograph of the little white dog on a navy couch is the identity reference. Draw this specific dog, preserving the recognizable proportions, face, coat, ears, and playful expression.
Primary request: a charming full-body hand-inked cartoon portrait of the photographed dog, seated and facing the viewer, matching the site's editorial cartoon style with crisp dark outlines, flat color, and delicate pencil hatch fur detail.
Subject: very small fluffy all-white Pomeranian/pomsky with subtly warm ivory tips around the ears, upright small triangular ears with pale pink inner ears, a soft round fluffy head, large round glossy dark eyes, tiny dark brown-black nose, short delicate muzzle, and a little pink tongue curling upward over the lower muzzle as in the photo. Mostly white fur, no gray mask, no tan patches, no black fur markings. Slender little front legs and small white paws, fluffy chest, tiny fluffy white tail peeking out on the dog's left/viewer's right. Match the dog's compact, fine-boned proportions in the photo, not a large husky.
Composition: centered full-body sitting pose, head facing directly forward, ears and both front paws fully visible, paws on one baseline, minimal equal margins, portrait canvas about 5:6 aspect ratio. Fit as a sprite in an existing 165 by 198 pixel webpage slot. Dog alone; no sofa, cushion, lamp, room, collar, ground plane, or objects.
Style: lively black-ink editorial cartoon, confident outer contour and sparse fine fur hatching, restrained creamy shading so the coat reads unmistakably WHITE. Not photorealistic, no 3D. Readable and cute at small size.
Background: genuinely transparent alpha, no white rectangle, no checkerboard drawn into the image, no exterior cast shadow.
No text, no watermark.
